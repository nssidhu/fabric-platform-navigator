Why the split matters

An ERP outage can pause destination updates without blocking Silver/Gold processing. Failed work remains visible and replayable through the dispatch layer.

Adapted Design Controls

Operational Guardrails

01Idempotent Delivery

Treat execution as at-least-once. Every destination write needs a stable idempotency key.

02Version-Bound Mapping

Bind each work item to its mapping version so a retry cannot silently adopt a changed transformation.

03State Reconciliation

Prove source intent equals destination state; a worker-reported success is not sufficient evidence.

04Safe Queue Claims

Use leasing, atomic status transitions, attempt limits, and poison-record handling for competing workers.

05Pointer Integrity

Bronze retention and immutable record identifiers must protect dispatch pointers from becoming invalid.

06Backpressure Control

Destination outages, ERP throttling, and queue growth require capacity controls—not only alerts.
